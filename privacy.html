Privacy Policy

This application is a personal rclone setup used by a single person to access their own Google Drive.

  • Google Drive data is accessed only on the owner's computer, through the Google Drive API.
  • No data is sent to, stored by, or shared with any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only in the local rclone configuration file.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account settings (Security > Third-party apps).
